Why Valtrices

Rank tells you where you are. Valtrices tells you why.

The in-game career tab stops at the scoreboard. Valtrices keeps the full picture across every match you play.

01

Everything in one place

Your matches, agents, maps, and stats live together in a single desktop app, with history that does not disappear after a handful of games.

02

Insight, not just numbers

Trends and match analysis connect the dots between games, so you can tell a bad night from a real pattern.

03

A score that reflects you

The custom performance score measures your contribution to a match, not just whether your team won, so improvement stays visible even on a losing streak.

Features

Everything you need to understand your game

From a quick look at last night's session to a deep dive into a single round, Valtrices is built around the questions players actually ask.

Match history

Every match you play is saved automatically with map, agent, mode, result, score, and date. Filter by any of them and jump back to a game whenever you want.

Detailed player statistics

Kills, deaths, assists, K/D, headshot rate, damage, first bloods, and more, broken down per match and across any range of games.

Agent & map statistics

Win rates and performance for every agent and map you play. See where you are strongest, where you struggle, and what to lock next.

Performance trends

Charts that follow your key stats over time. Spot a slump early, confirm that practice is paying off, and compare sessions at a glance.

Match analysis

Open any match for a closer look: the full scoreboard, round outcomes, and the moments that decided the game.

Custom performance score

A single rating that combines combat, impact, and consistency into one fair number for how you played, win or lose.

How the score works →
Performance score

One number that shows how you actually played

Rank goes up and down with your team. The Valtrices performance score looks only at what you did in the match, so a great game in a loss still counts, and a carried win does not hide a bad one.

  • Built from your match data. Combat, round impact, and consistency are weighed together into a single 0 to 100 score.
  • Comparable over time. It is calculated the same way for every match, so you can track it across sessions, agents, and maps.
  • Independent of the result. Your score reflects your contribution, not your teammates' performance.

How it works

Up and running in a few minutes

No spreadsheets and no manual tracking. Install once and Valtrices takes care of the rest.

  1. 1

    Download and install

    Get the latest Windows build from GitHub Releases and run the installer.

  2. 2

    Link your VALORANT account

    Connect your account inside the app so Valtrices can pick up your matches as you play.

  3. 3

    Play, then review

    After each session, open Valtrices to see new matches, updated stats, trends, and your latest performance score.
